ABSTRACT 
 
The TexMart – Smart Textile Management System is designed to streamline and modernize 
the management of textile businesses by addressing the limitations of traditional manual and 
semi-digital processes. Textile businesses often face challenges in maintaini ng inventory 
records, tracking sales, managing suppliers, and analyzing business performance efficiently. 
These issues can lead to errors, delays, and reduced productivity, ultimately affecting overall 
business growth and customer satisfaction.  The proposed system provides a comprehensive 
and user-friendly digital platform that enables efficient management of textile operations. It 
offers features such as real-time inventory tracking, automated billing, supplier management, 
and detailed sales analytics. The  system allows users to easily update stock, monitor product 
availability, and generate reports for better decision -making. Additionally, it enhances 
operational efficiency by reducing manual workload and minimizing errors through 
automation. TexMart is de veloped using modern web technologies, including HTML, CSS, 
JavaScript, and React for the front end, along with Node.js and Express.js for the back end. 
MongoDB is used for efficient data storage and retrieval. The system ensures data security, 
scalability, and high performance, making it suitable for both small -scale and large -scale 
textile businesses. Overall, TexMart provides an effective solution to improve productivity, 
accuracy, and business insights in the textile industry.

The “TEXMART – SMART TEXTILE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM”  is a user -

operations are simplified through technology, enhancing productivity, accuracy, and overall 
business growth.

manage inventory, sales, and supplier details. These systems provide only simple record -

business performance. Information is usually scattered, making it difficult to access and 
manage. These limitations reduce productivity and slow down decision -making. Businesses 
struggle to adapt to modern demands  due to lack of automation. Overall, such systems are 
outdated and not suitable for efficient textile management.

MAPPING OF PROJECT TO SDG 
 
SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth 
    The proposed TexMart – Smart Textile Management System strongly supports SDG 8: 
Decent Work and Economic Growth. The system enhances operational efficiency in textile 
management by enabling effective tracking of inventory, orders, production processes, and 
workforce activities. It helps reduce manual work, min imize errors, and improve overall 
accountability within the organization. By streamlining textile workflows, optimizing resource 
utilization, and ensuring timely production and delivery, the system contributes to increased 
productivity, better working conditions, and sustainable economic growth in the textile sector. 
SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure 
    The project also aligns with SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure by introducing 
a centralized digital platform for managing textile operations such as inventory, production, 
orders, and reporting. It promotes innovation through the use of modern web technologies like 
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL. The system supports digital transformation in the 
textile industry by replacing traditional manual processes with an automated, efficient, and 
scalable infrastructure, improving overall operational performance and reliability. 
SDG 16 – Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions 
    This project also supports SDG 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions by enhancing 
transparency, accountability, and structured workflow management within the Texmart textile 
system. Features such as inventory tracking, order status updates, production  monitoring, and 
reporting dashboards help maintain clear records and responsibility tracking across operations. 
This contributes to stronger organizational processes, improved decision -making, and better 
governance within the textile industry.
